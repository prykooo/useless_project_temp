How "Who Pays" Works

👥

1. Add Your Squad

Add all your friends at the table and enter the total bill amount. No real money needed!

🃏

2. Mystery Cards

Everyone is dealt a secret mystery card. Flip to discover your superpower or impending doom.

🎡

3. Roulette Spin

Spin the wheel! Watch out: LUCKY card holders get smaller wheel slices, tipping odds in their favor.

💥

4. Card Effects

Trigger SAFE re-spins, SWAP the bill to another friend, or pay DOUBLE! Winner gets crowned on the leaderboard.

🃏 The 4 Mystery Cards

Every Card Changes The Game

These aren't just cosmetic badges. Every card has a concrete mechanical impact on the final outcome!

🛡️
SAFE
Escape Shield
“ഇന്ന് നീ രക്ഷപ്പെട്ടു!”
If selected, you dodge paying completely and the roulette wheel spins again!
🍀
LUCKY
Weighted Odds
“ഭാഗ്യം ഇന്നും കൂടെയുണ്ട്!”
Shrinks your slice on the roulette wheel by 65%, giving you a much higher chance to avoid being picked.
🔄
SWAP
Uno Reverse
“ഇത് നിനക്ക് വേണ്ട… മറ്റൊരാൾക്ക് കൊടുക്കാം!”
If the wheel lands on you, pass the bill directly to any friend of your choice!
💀
DOUBLE
Financial Doom
“ഇന്നത്തെ ബിൽ… ഇരട്ടിയായി!”
